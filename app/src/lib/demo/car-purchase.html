Car purchase

One car to replace the old hatchback. Prices are rough used-market asks in EUR, 2 to 4 years old.

City

Cheap to own, easy to park. Enough if the long trips stay rare.

Toyota Yaris Hybrid

€16–21k

The default small hybrid. Sips fuel in town, and Toyota's hybrids have a long record of not breaking.

  • 4 l/100 km in the city
  • Holds its value
  • Small boot, tight back seat
  • Noisy on the motorway
Wikipedia

Hyundai i10

€11–15k

The budget option. A plain petrol engine, a 5-year warranty from new, and nothing to go wrong.

  • Cheapest to buy and insure
  • Surprisingly roomy for its size
  • Slow with four people aboard
  • Basic interior
Wikipedia

Family

Estates, for the bikes, the dog and the holiday luggage all at once.

Škoda Octavia Combi

€22–30k

The sensible estate. VW Group underneath, more space than a Golf, and the whole family fits with luggage to spare.

  • 640-litre boot
  • Diesel does 1,000 km on a tank
  • Fiddly touchscreen controls
  • Dull to drive
Wikipedia

Volvo V60

€28–38k

The nice estate. Best seats in the class, a calm interior, and a plug-in version that covers the commute on electricity.

  • Comfort and safety kit
  • Plug-in hybrid option
  • Servicing costs
  • Smaller boot than the Octavia
Wikipedia

Electric

Only if the landlord agrees to a charger in the garage. Ask first.

Hyundai Ioniq 5

€30–40k

Fast charging and a flat floor make it the most practical EV here. Looks like a concept car, drives like a sofa.

  • 10 to 80% in 18 minutes
  • Huge cabin
  • Wide for old car parks
  • Range drops hard in winter
Wikipedia

Volkswagen ID.3

€20–27k

The Golf-sized EV. Early cars are cheap now because the software was rough; later ones fixed most of it.

  • Compact, easy to park
  • Plenty on the used market
  • Check the software version
  • Cheap interior plastics
Wikipedia

Weekend

For the mountain cabin and the forest tracks. The heart's pick, not the head's.

Suzuki Jimny

€22–28k

A tiny proper off-roader with a ladder frame and low-range gears. Everyone smiles at it. Terrible on the motorway, and I don't care.

  • Goes anywhere
  • Prices barely drop
  • Two usable seats
  • Loud and thirsty at 120 km/h
Wikipedia

Dacia Duster 4x4

€17–24k

Half the price of the obvious SUVs, with real four-wheel drive and a roof rack. The compromise that does family and weekend both.

  • Value, space, 4x4
  • Cheap parts
  • Firm ride
  • Resale weaker than the Jimny
Wikipedia
