# Asia trip

Six weeks, early November to mid-December: the dry season in Vietnam and Cambodia, shoulder season in Thailand. Three bases rather than a new city every other day, with a slow week in the middle.

## Route

1. **Bangkok**, 4 nights. Land, adjust, eat.
2. **Chiang Mai**, 6 nights. Cooking class, a day at an elephant sanctuary, Doi Suthep at sunrise.
3. **Hanoi**, 3 nights, then the overnight train to **Da Nang** and 7 nights in **Hội An**. Tailors, the beach, a Hải Vân Pass scooter day.
4. **Siem Reap**, 4 nights. Angkor over two days with a rest day between.
5. **Koh Lanta**, 8 nights. The slow week: nothing booked.
6. Back to **Bangkok** for the flight home.

## Booked

- Flights out and back, aisle seats
- Bangkok hotel, first 4 nights
- Overnight train Hanoi → Da Nang, soft sleeper

## To sort out

- Vietnam e-visa, apply 3 weeks before
- Cambodia e-visa, apply 1 week before
- Travel insurance with scooter cover
- Tell the bank, order a second card
- eSIM for the first days, local SIM after

## Budget

Around €70 a day outside flights: €25 room, €15 food, €10 transport, the rest for tours and the odd nice dinner. Thailand costs more than Vietnam, Cambodia sits in between.

## Packing notes

- Light rain jacket for the north of Vietnam
- Long sleeves and trousers for temples
- Leave room: tailored shirts from Hội An
